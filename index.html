HELPFUL ANSWERS

Questions? We can help.

What does FIRECO do?

We supply, install and maintain fire alarm and firefighting systems, offer annual maintenance contracts (AMC), and assist with UAE Civil Defence certificate documentation and application follow-up.

What types of properties do you serve?

Our services support residential, commercial and industrial buildings and properties across the UAE.

Which emirates do you serve?

Our seven offices serve all seven emirates: Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah.

Can you source equipment from different suppliers?

Yes. We buy from different suppliers according to project requirements. Send your equipment list and system specifications to discuss supply options.

How do I request a quotation?

Call our team or email your building location and required services to info@firecots.com.
